Each frame, one compute shader walks every check-in, bins it into screen-space tiles with atomic adds, and builds the three linked histograms in workgroup memory. A fragment shader then colors each pixel from its bin, the same two passes as the paper's Figure 8. deck.gl owns the camera and draws the label and brush layers on top.

Click a histogram bar to isolate it, drag across bars to brush a range, click again to clear. The data is synthetic, modeled on the paper's Brightkite check-ins (heavy in Denver and the Bay Area, seasonal in Florida and the north), because the real SNAP file can't load inside this page.
