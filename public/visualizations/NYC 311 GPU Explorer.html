Every frame, a compute shader filters every request against all four histograms and the map brush, bins the survivors into screen tiles with atomic adds, and rebuilds the cross-filtered histograms in workgroup memory. A fragment shader then colors each pixel by its bin. deck.gl owns the camera and draws the labels and brush.

Try Heat/Hot Water and drag the month chart through winter, or pick the three noise types and narrow the hours to 10 pm to 2 am.

The requests are synthetic, because NYC Open Data can't be reached from this page. Type shares, places, hours and seasons are modeled on the real 311 feed, and the shares are approximate.
