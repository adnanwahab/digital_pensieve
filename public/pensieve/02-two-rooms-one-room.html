Two Rooms, One Room

You both remember the same rooms, but not the same way. Each of you sees only your own version. Talk it through, find the seven things that differ, and mark each one together.

Player A · gold

W S walk, A D turn, hold F to place your marker. Left half of the screen.

Player B · blue

↑ ↓ walk, ← → turn, hold Enter to place your marker. Right half.

  1. Stand at a difference, face it, and both hold your marker keys for one second.
  2. Something missing on your side? Face the empty spot and mark it anyway.
  3. Find all seven and the door opens. Linger too long and the memory blurs, then lets you go.

Or both hold your marker keys together to dive. Play fair: watch only your own half.

This memory needs a keyboard shared by two players.
