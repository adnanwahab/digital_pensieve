    HOW TO HOLD ON

    Take turns being the anchor.

    Sol falls down. Lune falls up. Your cord stretches, but neither of you can leave the other behind.

    1. Find a higher hold. Move around the edge of a shelf to rise past it. Press anchor near any surface to stay there. A small halo shows when a hold is within reach. Dream shelves catch your falling side; you can jump or pull through their backs.
    2. Bring your partner with you. Jump opposite your gravity. When your partner is anchored, hold jump to pull yourself toward them. Steer left or right to swing. Tap anchor again to let go.
    3. Meet inside each silver circle. Both of you must be inside together. Six circles lead through the memory. In the upside-down library, your gravity swaps.
