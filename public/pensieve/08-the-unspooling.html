A SMALL GUIDE TO IMPOSSIBLE THINGS

Hold the moment.

The Clockwinder moves the room backward and forward through 24 seconds. The Walker moves freely and never rewinds.

01

Make a path. Drag the timeline to rebuild, open, and unfold the way ahead. Pause at a useful moment.

02

Walk through it. Use WASD, then E near a glowing memory or the exit. You can also click the ground to walk.

03

Keep what you find. Collected memories stay with you through every rewind. Refuge platforms are always safe.

Solo: use both sets of controls. Shared keyboard: Player A walks with WASD + E; Player B winds time with ← → and Space. Watch the role labels if the clock changes its mind.
