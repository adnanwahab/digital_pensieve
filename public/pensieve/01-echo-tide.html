Echo Tide

A gold memory: the Diver's kitchen at dusk. One of you stands inside it. The other sees only smeared color on the water.

Diver

Walk the memory and describe it: what is where, how big, near the door or far from it.

Shore

Place objects from a tray of 20 onto the water. Twelve belong. Eight are decoys. Size them to match the blurs.

  • Three rounds of 90 seconds. Talk the whole time.
  • After each round the scenes overlay. Objects within about a step of the right spot, at about the right size, lock in.
  • The Diver sees the Shore's placements as blue outlines.
  • Each round, one object in the memory quietly changes. If the Diver taps it after it changes and marks it, you earn a bonus thread.

Sharing one screen? The Shore player should keep their eyes on the right-hand pane, or use the Diver and Shore views on two devices side by side.
