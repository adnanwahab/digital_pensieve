Mirror Walk

Every step you take is mirrored in your partner's maze. Your own avatar moves the way you press; your partner's moves the opposite way across the mirror.

  • Doors open together. Both avatars must step onto their glowing door tiles within half a second of each other.
  • Walls don't reset you. Bumping one costs a little clarity and blurs the dream.
  • Watch the tide line. From memory 4 on, past the teal line the mirror turns from left–right to up–down.
  • Lockstep. Inputs are gathered each 100 ms tick and applied to both mazes at once.

Gold: W A S D   Blue: arrow keys   On a phone, use the two pads.
