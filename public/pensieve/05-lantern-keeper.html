Lantern Keeper

One of you has the light. The other has the map. The house moves when no one is looking.

Keeper · keyboard

Walk the dark house with WASD. Space raises the lantern: more light, faster drain. E opens a dresser for oil. You never see the map.

Reader · mouse

You see the floor plan and the stairs. Click to drop up to 3 stars; they show as faint lights for the Keeper. Your map only updates where the lantern shines.

Three floors, five minutes each. Furniture in unlit rooms slowly rearranges itself, so old ink on the map may be wrong. Nothing ends in a loss: run out of oil and the lantern shrinks to an ember; run out of time and the floor dissolves beneath you. Clarity comes from floors cleared and oil left. Talk to each other. If you share one screen, the Reader can tick “Shield map”.
